# Getting started

STORM helps security teams manage assessment projects: keep track of target hosts and services, record findings, share notes, and generate Word reports.

## Getting access

There is no public registration. An administrator invites you by email:

1. Open the invitation link.
2. Choose your username or full name; availability is checked automatically.
3. Set and confirm your password.
4. Activate the account.

## Signing in

Open the STORM sign-in page and enter your username and password. If two-factor authentication is enabled, enter the six-digit code from your authenticator app.

Sessions use secure cookies and refresh automatically while you work. You may need to sign in again after a long absence.

## Finding your way around

The sidebar contains:

- **Projects** — assessments you can access, and the main starting point.
- **Tasks** — tasks from the configured Task Tracker integration.
- **Docs** — this help section.
- **Members** — workspace user management, available to administrators.

The bell opens notifications. Click your name in the top right to open **Profile** or **Logout**. The arrow in the sidebar header collapses the sidebar.

Choose the interface language and theme under **Profile → Customizing**. The saved language is also used on public account screens.

## Next steps

See **Projects**, **Hosts and assets**, and **Vulnerabilities** for assessment workflows. Ordinary users see projects where they are members. If your list is empty, ask a project manager to add you.
